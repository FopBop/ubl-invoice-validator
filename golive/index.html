Frequently asked

What do I actually pay for if the validator is open source?

The rules are public and the core is free. You pay for not having to run and maintain a validator: a hosted endpoint, bulk archive validation, rule updates, and an accountable support contact.

Does the free tier upload my invoices?

No. The free browser validator runs entirely client-side — nothing is uploaded, and there is no tracking. The hosted plans exist for teams that prefer a server-side endpoint.

Is this a certification?

No. It is a linter for a documented subset of EN 16931 and Peppol BIS rules. It lowers the most common rejection causes but does not guarantee acceptance by every access point.

How do I pay?

Pro is billed monthly and Enterprise annually. Payment is settled in USDC on Base (chain id 8453); details are shared when you get in touch. No billing system or card details are needed for the first customers.
