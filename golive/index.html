Pricing questions

Is the free version really free, or is it a trial?

Really free, and MIT-licensed. There is no time limit and no signup. The free CLI, library, and browser checker will always work — that is a hard commitment, not a marketing line.

What am I paying for in Pro if the engine is the same?

You are paying for operation: an EU-hosted endpoint you don't run, batch handling, and rule updates maintained for you. If you are happy running the CLI yourself, stay on Free — honestly, stay on Free.

Does passing validation guarantee my invoice is accepted?

No. This is a linter for a documented subset of rules, not a certification body. You are paying for integration and accountability, not a false promise of universal acceptance.

Can I self-host Enterprise?

Yes — Enterprise is exactly that: on-prem or private deployment, with custom CIUS rule packs and an integration review, priced per integration rather than per seat.

Do you lock me in?

No. The free engine is yours under MIT. If you leave, your validator keeps working.
