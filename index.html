What it checks — and what it won't

A focused subset of the rules that cause most real-world access-point rejections. Each violation is reported as a rule code (e.g. BR-03a), the location, and a one-line fix.

EN 16931 core rules

Mandatory business terms and the BR-01…BR-09 presence/format checks — invoice number, issue date (ISO 8601), currency (ISO 4217), seller identity, and totals that reconcile.

Peppol BIS 3.0 profile

Detects the CustomizationID and applies profile rules — for example the seller contact e-mail required by Peppol BIS (PEPPOL-001).

Totals reconciliation

Catches the silent killer: TaxExclusiveAmount that doesn't equal the sum of line amounts (BR-CO-13) — a mismatch that gets the invoice bounced.

Honest scope: this is a linter for a documented subset, not a certification body. Passing it lowers the most common rejection causes but does not guarantee acceptance by every access point.

Frequently asked

Does my invoice get uploaded anywhere?

No. Validation runs entirely in your browser in JavaScript. Your XML never leaves your device, and there is no tracking or account.

Which e-invoice formats are supported?

UBL 2.1 Invoice and UN/CEFACT CII CrossIndustryInvoice XML — the formats used by Peppol BIS and XRechnung.

What is EN 16931?

The European standard defining the semantic model and business rules for a core e-invoice. It makes invoices interoperable across the EU, Peppol, and national profiles (CIUS) such as XRechnung.

Can I run this in CI or on a server?

Yes. The same rule subset ships as a zero-dependency Python CLI (ublcheck.py) and a small HTTP service (POST /validate, plus bulk POST /validate-batch).
